White Water Warrior Challenge

Intermediate 3-4 hours $129/person Groups of 6-12

For those with some rafting experience seeking more excitement! Tackle Class III-IV rapids through challenging river sections. This trip includes technical maneuvering, bigger waves, and the thrill of navigating through narrow passages. Previous rafting experience recommended.

  • Advanced safety equipment and briefing
  • Expert guides with swift water rescue certification
  • Action photography package included
  • Riverside picnic lunch
  • Minimum age: 14 years
  • Technical skills training provided
  • GoPro mount available for personal cameras
Highlights: Class III-IV rapids, technical challenges, action-packed adventure

The ultimate adrenaline rush for experienced rafters! Navigate Class V rapids on our most challenging route. This full-day expedition includes technical rapids, cliff jumping opportunities, and wilderness survival tips. Requires previous white water rafting experience and good physical fitness.

  • Elite safety gear and comprehensive safety briefing
  • Master guides with 10+ years experience
  • Professional video footage with GoPro cameras
  • Gourmet riverside lunch prepared by guide
  • Minimum age: 18 years
  • Wilderness survival demonstration
  • Cliff jumping opportunities (optional)
  • Certificate of completion
Highlights: Class V extreme rapids, full-day expedition, cliff jumping, wilderness experience
